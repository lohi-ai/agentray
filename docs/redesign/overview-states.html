How these numbers are computed

Active people: distinct canonical people with qualifying human product activity; bot, test, internal and server-only background events excluded. New people: first observed qualifying activity across retained history — not signup or download. Sessions: one versioned session rule across SDK and server. Activation: project-chosen activation event within 24 hours of first activity. Revenue: requires a trusted billing source; unconfigured shows “Set up”, never zero.

What this overview will show you

Once events arrive, this page answers three questions without any setup: who is arriving, whether they activate, and whether they come back. When a pattern is worth acting on, a Best next step panel appears here with the evidence behind it — the metric, its range, and its freshness.

Example — not your data. “Activation fell from 31% to 25% this week; 80 of 320 new users finished setup.” A real finding replaces this once your data lands.
