From analysis to a measurable marketing plan

Help new users finish their first project

Example hypothesis · draft

Observation: 80 of 320 new users completed a project within 24 hours. This identifies a possible opportunity; it does not explain the cause.

Next investigation: compare activation by source and platform, then inspect where users stop.

Proposed experiment: test a shorter first-project guide with eligible new signups. Measure 24-hour activation; monitor support contacts and early retention as guardrails.

A saved plan includes baseline, evidence, audience, owner, review date and outcome. Publishing or sending is a later capability.
