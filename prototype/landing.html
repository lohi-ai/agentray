The loop

Measure, diagnose, test, learn — shipped as the product.

  1. 01 / MEASURE

    It reads your product data.

    The events you already send. The same store that powers your charts is the one the agent reads.

  2. 02 / DIAGNOSE

    It names one step, not ten charts.

    You get “activation → conversion is your widest gap”, not a board to interpret.

  3. 03 / TEST

    It designs the test.

    The smallest experiment that would move that step, and what to measure.

  4. 04 / LEARN

    It remembers what you learned.

    Last cycle’s result is in next cycle’s reasoning. A dashboard starts over every time.
